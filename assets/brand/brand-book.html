01 · The briefBrief and references

A free desktop app where you practise spoken English with Claude, and nobody corrects you mid-sentence.

After each conversation: one focus and at most two smaller points, in your own language. It remembers what you repeat until you master it in real conversation, and teaches the words of your books.

02 · The ideaThe ear

Ogmios led his listeners by chains of gold and amber, from his tongue to their ears.

The symbol is where that chain arrives. One squared line winds inwards like an ear and is never cut, the way you talk in Ogmios without being stopped. At its centre: one amber square, the one focus you take away.

— The myth without the figure: no god, no chain drawn, only where it lands and its gold.

— Construction: one line of width u = 26 on a 256 grid, its centre-lines 44 apart, square joins, an open start.

— The square is 32, a little more than u, so it reads as the line's destination.
